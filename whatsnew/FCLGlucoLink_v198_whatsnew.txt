Fixed a bug where the Dexcom G7/ONE+ connection could occasionally read the same measurement twice in a row, or (especially right after pairing on a new phone) stop connecting altogether. This could show up in AAPS as two slightly different values at the same time, which made AAPS turn the loop off as a safety precaution. Reconnect timing is now much more robust against small and larger timing variations, so this shouldn't happen anymore. Also improved reliability when running two sensors (e.g. G6 + G7) at the same time: they no longer open a Bluetooth connection at the exact same moment, which could otherwise make the G7 fail to connect, and the G7's own reconnect timing now proactively steers clear of the other sensor's schedule so it stops skipping roughly every other reading. 

Also fixed the "About & update" button being invisible on some phones with a larger system font size.
