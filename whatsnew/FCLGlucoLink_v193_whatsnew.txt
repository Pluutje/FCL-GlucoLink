Fixed the real cause of the CareSens Air crash loop from the last update: the previous fix compared too much of the sensor's data, including two numbers that always change, so it could never recognize a repeat of the record that crashed the app. That comparison is now limited to the actual measurement, so a genuinely stuck record is correctly skipped instead of crashing the app over and over.

Added a new "Diagnostics" section to every sensor's status screen, and a short one-line summary on the home screen. It now shows how long the sensor has been running, and whether its readings currently look normal or unusual. If several readings in a row come back exactly the same, the app says so in plain language, since real sensors always have a small amount of natural variation — a long run of identical values usually means something is wrong with the connection or the sensor, not that your glucose is perfectly flat. For CareSens Air specifically, the app now also explains in plain language when the sensor itself reports a problem, or when it hasn't produced a real reading in a while, so it's clearer whether an issue is coming from the app or from the sensor.

The daily log file now also records which app version wrote it, making it easier to match a reported problem to the exact version that was running.
