Alarm pause: the pause is now saved and shown correctly (red banner and crossed-out bell). Snooze and stop lines on the main screen have an "End" button. Buttons in the pause and snooze sliders no longer jump around.
Alarms: a snoozed or stopped alarm now also mutes the less severe alarms in the same direction (Low mutes Predictive Low). Urgent Low and Urgent High still come through. New: pencil on the glucose chart to set the chart height.
Main screen: smal pencil on the chart to adjust heigt and a slightly more compact BG circle and spacing.
