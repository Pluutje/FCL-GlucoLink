Widened the CareSens Air stuck-data recovery added in the previous update. It previously only kicked in when the sensor announced "0 new records" for too long. A second real-world case showed the same frozen-data problem while the sensor kept announcing a growing (non-zero) count of new records. The app now watches the time since the last real measurement instead, regardless of what the sensor announces, so this kind of stall is caught either way.
