Fixed a bug where the Dexcom G7/ONE+ connection could occasionally read the same measurement twice in a row (most noticeable when your phone's screen was in sleep/battery-saving mode). This could show up in AAPS as two slightly different values at the same time, which made AAPS turn the loop off as a safety precaution. Reconnect timing is now more tolerant of small timing variations, so this shouldn't happen anymore.
