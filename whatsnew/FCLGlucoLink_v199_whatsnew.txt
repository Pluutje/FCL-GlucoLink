Added a Dutch translation. Go to Settings > Language to switch between English and Nederlands at any time — English stays the default, so nothing changes unless you switch it yourself. The Manual, the Settings screen, the sensor picker, the Combi overview, and the Calibration screen are now fully available in Dutch; technical sensor-diagnostics wording (sensor life, battery voltage, temperature, and similar fields, mostly on the sensor status screens) stays in English on purpose, since those are technical terms rather than everyday text.
