FSL3 filter (TEST): made calmer. The default is now smoothing 0.15 with no lead, so the Libre 3 is about as calm as the Dexcom G6. The smoothing slider now goes from 0.05 to 1.00 (about 0.10 is as calm as the G6 after its own filtering). If you set your own values earlier, press "Reset to defaults".
